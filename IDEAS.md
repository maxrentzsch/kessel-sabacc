# Ideas

Things that could still be added. Effort is a rough guess: small / medium / large. The larger
plan, in phases, is in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).

## Next

- **Corellian Spike beyond free play** (medium): career tables, tour stops and daily hands of its own.
- **More Sabacc variants** such as Coruscant Shift or classic Sabacc (large).

## Game modes

- **Puzzle hands** (medium): given situations with exactly one winning move.
- **Tournament** (medium): three tables in a row, chips carry over, a final table at the end.
- **Two players on one device** (medium): pass the phone, the other hand stays hidden.
- **Watch and bet** (medium): AI opponents play each other, put credits on the winner.
- **Blitz Sabacc** (small): a time limit per turn.
- **Online against others** (large, needs a server).

## Depth

- **Tells** (medium): every opponent gives a strong hand away in their own way.
- **Opponent dossiers** (small to medium): a file per opponent with your record, their tokens and
  the tells you have found.
- **More cheating** (medium): swap a card from your sleeve, at a high cost in suspicion.
- **Card counter** (small): shows which cards have gone, unlocked as a ship module.

## Long-term goals

- **Contracts** (medium): three small goals a day and a week.
- **Crew** (medium): beaten rivals sign on to the Sandrunner and bring small perks.
- **Share the daily hand** (small): the result as a short text to send.

## Presentation

- **Square chips at some venues** (small), like at the first table in the original.
- **Music** (medium): needs real audio files.
- **Impostor and Sylop art closer to the original** (small): needs reference images.

## Comfort

- **Left-handed layout and a colour-blind mode** (small).
- **Look up the rules at the table** (small).
